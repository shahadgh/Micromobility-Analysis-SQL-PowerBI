# Micromobility-Analysis-SQL-PowerBI
A data analytics project focusing on micromobility revenue, profitability, and rider behavior. This project develops a comprehensive dashboard using operational data to inform critical business decisions, including optimal pricing and operational efficiency. Key analysis includes Price Elasticity of Demand (PED) and seasonal trends.






**Performance & Pricing Overview (2021 to 2022)**

The Scooter service demonstrated significant growth despite a substantial price increase.

•	Price Increase: The Average Price per ride increased from $3.99 in 2021 to $4.99 in 2022, representing a 25% price hike.

•	Ridership Growth: Despite the price increase, Riders increased substantially, rising from 1.24 Million to 2.05 Million (a 65% increase).

•	Profit Improvement: Profit also saw a significant improvement (specific percentage is redacted but growth is implied as substantial). This successful performance suggests that riders were relatively insensitive to the price change last year, likely due to external growth.

**Price Elasticity of Demand (PED) Interpretation**

The calculated Price Elasticity of Demand provides a critical economic context for future strategy.

•	PED Value: The Price Elasticity of Demand (PED) for the service is approximately 2.21.

•	Elastic Demand: Since the absolute value of the PED (2.21) is greater than 1, the demand is considered price elastic. In pure economic terms, this means customers are highly responsive, and a price change causes a proportionally larger change in ridership.

•	Key Insight (Non-Price Factors): The fact that both price and ridership increased is highly unusual for an elastic good. This suggests the demand was strongly pushed up by non-price factors (e.g., market expansion, better service, or marketing) which outweighed the negative effect of the price increase.

**Recommendation & Price Target**

Future price adjustments must be cautious to avoid hitting a price sensitivity threshold.

•	Recommendation: Implement a moderate price increase that is lower than the 25% increase from 2021 to 2022.

•	Specific Target: Target a 10% price increase for the upcoming year (2023), setting the New Average Price Target at $5.49 (from $\$4.99$).


________________________________________
**This moderate increase aims to:**

o	Maintain the current high profitability.

o	Avoid alienating the large new rider base.

o	Keep the service competitive while accounting for inflation and operational costs.
